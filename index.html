01 / Deck contents

Five parts, one page.

Every deck follows the same structure, so a sales manager knows exactly where to click. You fill it in once, then keep adding calls and results as you book them. No page builder, no theme picking, no dead links.

01 / Calls

Unlimited mock calls

Save as many recordings as you want: discovery, objection handling, the full close. Label each one with the offer, the price point, and the outcome so a reviewer can pick the call that matters to them.

02 / Proof

Proof of performance

Upload CRM screenshots, commission statements, Stripe dashboards, and leaderboard photos. Add a caption to each image so the numbers have context instead of sitting there unexplained.

03 / Bio

Bio and summary

A short intro at the top, then a longer summary covering the offers you have sold, ticket sizes, closing rates, and the sales process you run best in.

04 / Resume

Attached resume

Keep your PDF where recruiters expect it. It downloads in one click from the same page as the calls, so nobody has to email you asking for it.

05 / Link

One shareable URL

Paste it in a job application, a DM, or your LinkedIn headline. Update the deck later and the link stays the same.

02 / Build sequence

How a deck comes together.

  1. 01

    Claim your page

    Pick a handle, add a headshot, and write the two-line bio a hiring manager reads first. That is the whole setup step.

  2. 02

    Upload the calls

    Drop in audio or video from your mock calls and real recorded sessions. Order them so your strongest close sits at the top, and tag each with the offer and ticket size.

  3. 03

    Add the receipts

    Screenshots of pipeline, cash collected, and closing percentage go in the proof section. Blur what you need to. Captions carry the context.

  4. 04

    Send the link

    One URL replaces the attachment pile. Applications, cold outreach, referral intros: same link every time, always current.
