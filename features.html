01 / Building blocks

Four blocks.
One link a hiring
manager will open.

A resume says you closed. A deck shows it. Every block below is built around the way sales managers actually screen remote closers: they skim, they click one recording, they check the numbers, then they decide whether to book you.

Spec sheet follows

01 / Calls

Unlimited mock call recordings

Upload every roleplay, discovery run, and objection drill you record. There is no cap and no archive fee, so the weak early attempts can sit next to the sharp recent ones if you want the growth on display.

Fields
Title, scenario, ticket size, date
Sources
Loom, Drive, Zoom, direct upload
Order
Pin your best call to the top
Access
Public link or private per manager

02 / Proof

Proof-of-performance galleries

Commission statements, CRM dashboards, leaderboard placements, the Slack message where your manager called out the close. Drop the screenshots in, caption each one so the context is obvious, and reorder them by dragging.

Layout
Masonry grid, full-screen on tap
Privacy
Blur client names before you post

Sample gallery

03 / Bio

Bio and summary blocks

Two short blocks sit at the top of the deck. The bio is you in your own words, four or five lines, written the way you talk on a call. The summary is the hard read: offers you have sold, ticket range, industries, average cycle length, and where you are based.

Summary block, sample

Coaching and agency offers, $3k to $18k tickets. Inbound setter-to-closer handoff. 240 calls taken in the last quarter, cycle averaging 6 days. Central time, available for a 40 hour seat.

04 / Resume

An attachable resume that stays current

Attach the PDF once. When you update it, every link you have already sent points at the new version, so the manager who bookmarked your deck in March sees the file you uploaded last week. Downloads happen on the same page as your calls.

Format
PDF or DOCX, 20 MB limit
Versions
Replace anytime, link stays the same

02 / Workflow

Built in an afternoon, sent in a second

Most closers finish their first deck between two calls. The order below is the one we recommend, because it front-loads what a hiring manager checks first.

  1. 01

    Claim your deck link

    Pick a handle and you have a live URL. Nothing is public until you flip the switch, so you can build in the dark.

  2. 02

    Load the tape

    Paste recording links or upload files. Label the scenario and ticket size on each one, then pin the call you want watched first.

  3. 03

    Add proof and paperwork

    Screenshots into the gallery, resume into the attachment slot, bio and summary at the top. Captions do the explaining for you.

  4. 04

    Send one link

    Drop it in the application field, the DM, or the email reply. You see when it was opened and which call was played.
